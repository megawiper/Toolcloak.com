# Code of Conduct

> We want Toolcloak to be a friendly, respectful place to build and learn, for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or language.

## 💜 What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful.
- Assume good intent, and say when something is unclear.

## 🚫 What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discrimination or hateful language
- Sharing someone's private information
- Spam, scams or self-promotion unrelated to the project
- Anything illegal or unsafe

## 🌐 Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## 🔨 Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and restrict participation to protect the community.

To report a concern, message the maintainer **[@megawiper](https://github.com/megawiper)** on GitHub and ask for a private channel. Reports are handled with care and kept as private as possible.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
